## 5. Methodological note

"Closure at round k" claims by ANY budgeted searcher are artifacts in
BOTH directions: missed proofs inflate class counts (the four §40
witnesses were one class), and missed countermodels deflate them (the
≤4-world battery cannot separate {q9, q12, q14}).  The honest regime,
used here: two-sided certification, exhaustive small-frame sweeps with
a completeness argument (rooted generated-submodel reduction), bounded
targeted hunts beyond, and a hand-certificate layer for the
enumerable searcher-gap patterns (`ofImpTop` order facts, ex falso
inside ◯, ◯-intro under `orElim`, collapse-lemma cuts).
